# WILDCASE Demo Video Script
**Target Duration:** 60–90 Seconds  
**Tone:** Cinematic, mysterious, technical, restrained  
**Core Message:** The world is the case file. Put your phone away and investigate.

---

### Timeline & Storyboard

#### **0:00 – 0:05 | Hook & Title**
- **Visual:** Cinematic dark screen with amber phosphor glow. Fraunces editorial title reveals: `WILDCASE — THE WORLD IS THE CASE FILE.`
- **Audio:** Low atmospheric mechanical hum + typewriter audio cue.
- **Narration (Voiceover / ElevenLabs):**
  > *"What if your next walk wasn't a distraction, but a classified investigation?"*

---

#### **0:05 – 0:15 | Case Briefing**
- **Visual:** Player scrolls through active dossiers on mobile device. Selects **"CASE 014: The Silent Witness"**. Briefing unfolds with classified stamps, suspect cards (Evelyn Vance, Arthur Pendelton, Julian Mercer), and environmental target sector (Park Perimeter).
- **Audio:** Tactile paper shuffle sound effect.
- **Narration:**
  > *"A midnight incident. Three suspects. The evidence isn't on the internet—it's somewhere out in the physical world."*

---

#### **0:15 – 0:22 | The Anti-Screen Ritual: Field Mode**
- **Visual:** Player taps **"ENTER FIELD MODE"**. Screen transitions to pitch black HUD with a single amber pulsating sonar ring and text: **"PHONE IN POCKET — OBSERVE YOUR SURROUNDINGS"**.
- **Action:** Actor places the phone into their jacket pocket and steps outside onto a park trail.
- **Narration:**
  > *"Step one: Put the phone away. Stop looking at a screen and start observing your environment."*

---

#### **0:22 – 0:35 | Physical Exploration**
- **Visual:** Cut to actor walking along a real-world path, looking at fence lines, brick pillars, and tree bark. Sunlight filtering through foliage.
- **Audio:** Ambient footsteps on gravel, distant birds, soft synthesizer drone.
- **Narration:**
  > *"WILDCASE challenges you to search for real physical textures, materials, and structures."*

---

#### **0:35 – 0:48 | On-Device Evidence Scan**
- **Visual:** Actor spots an old rusted wrought-iron perimeter gate. Takes out phone. Opens camera HUD. Real-time overlay displays Sobel edge contours, luminance histograms, and detected tags: `[metal, vertical, rough, fence]`.
- **Audio:** High-tech camera focus chime + mechanical shutter snap.
- **Narration:**
  > *"When you find a match, draw your device for a 3-second scan. All vision processing happens on-device in browser memory. Zero photos are uploaded."*

---

#### **0:48 – 0:58 | Deterministic Verification & AI Interpretation**
- **Visual:** Green **"VERIFIED"** stamp slams onto the screen. AI detective voiceover triggers, narrating how the forced toolmarks contradict Evelyn Vance's master-key alibi. Suspect Evelyn is marked **ELIMINATED**.
- **Audio:** Heavy tactile stamp impact sound.
- **Narration:**
  > *"The core engine deterministically verifies the physical match, while the AI Game Master interprets the forensic meaning and updates your suspect board."*

---

#### **0:58 – 1:12 | Accusation & Verdict**
- **Visual:** Actor reviews final clue trail. Enters **"ACCUSATION ROOM"**. Selects Arthur Pendelton and chooses the motive/evidence chain. Taps **"SUBMIT FORMAL CHARGES"**.
- **Visual:** Screen fades to red-amber seal: **"CASE CLOSED — GUILTY VERDICT CONFIRMED"**.
- **Audio:** Dramatic orchestral resolution chord.
- **Narration:**
  > *"Piece the physical clues together, eliminate false leads, and deliver the final verdict."*

---

#### **1:12 – 1:25 | Field Report & Screen Time Metrics**
- **Visual:** The **Official Field Report** renders: Total Walk Time: 32m 30s. Away Ratio: **87.4% Away From Screen**. Evidence Log: 4/4 Verified.
- **Narration:**
  > *"Every case generates an immutable field report proving you spent over 85% of your time exploring the real world."*

---

#### **1:25 – 1:30 | Outro**
- **Visual:** Actor pocketing phone again, looking up at the park horizon.
- **Text on Screen:**
  ```
  WILDCASE
  The world is the case file.
  Built with React 19, Google Gemma, Mastra, MongoDB & ElevenLabs.
  ```
- **Narration:**
  > *"WILDCASE. The world is the case file."*
